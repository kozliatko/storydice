# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, ability, identity or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and be patient with questions.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private channel, such as a GitHub security advisory or the contact details on the maintainer's profile.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
